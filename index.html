R Calculator

R is the center of the universe. Method units, not dollars. You set the risk unit before you enter; the calculator returns the size, the stop, the target and the tally. It does not negotiate with mood, caffeination, or your sense of what is or isn't a large amount of money.

01 · Desk
1R recalculates from AUM, so it scales as you compound. Each trade locks its own R unit at the moment it opens. R expresses how much edge is in front of you — never the size you want. The survival levers (heat cap, cold-streak brake) sit outside per-trade sizing on purpose. Data lives in this browser only: use Export at the bottom to back it up.
